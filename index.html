Record: pick a pad, tap Record sample, make a sound, tap again to stop. Silence is trimmed, and every recording is kept in My Recordings. Library: hold any pad (or tap Sound library) to swap its sound. Stock sounds are always there to bring back. Stack: every pad is its own track, so all eight play together. Tap a name in the grid to mute it. Keys: play the selected sample chromatically. With Live on, what you play while the loop runs is written in. No sound on an iPhone? Turn the silent switch off.
